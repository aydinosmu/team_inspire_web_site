Team INSPIRE

Team INSPIRE focuses on sustainability, water purification, circular economy and youth-led scientific innovation. The team develops solutions that connect laboratory research with real-world environmental needs.

Global Recognition

After becoming a global winner, Team INSPIRE gained international recognition. Yusuf Aydın Doğru, the founder and team captain, was invited to the World Youth Development Forum in China.

Flagship Project

PureFlow

PureFlow is a sustainable water purification project that explores the use of processed waste mussel shells as an adsorbent filtration material for polluted water.

The project aims to create a low-cost, scalable and circular solution for water treatment, especially for pollution problems linked to industrial wastewater.

Project Focus

  • Waste mussel shell based filtration
  • Adsorption-focused purification approach
  • Low-cost and sustainable material design
  • Laboratory R&D and future prototype development
