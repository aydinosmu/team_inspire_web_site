About the Project

The PureFlow Revolution

PureFlow was developed by Team INSPIRE to address one of the world’s most urgent challenges: access to clean and safe water. The project repurposes discarded mussel shells and transforms them into an activated, porous adsorbent material.

Through carbonization and activation, the material develops a nanoporous structure with a large surface area. When contaminated water passes through this material, pollutant molecules attach to the pore surfaces through adsorption, allowing cleaner water to exit the system.

Adsorption & Cleaner Water

Key Scientific Note

Adsorption means pollutants attach to the surface of a material. In PureFlow, dye molecules and heavy metal particles are captured on the walls of nanopores, not absorbed into the bulk material.
