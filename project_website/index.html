The Flagship Project

PureFlow

A sustainable approach to industrial water purification.

The Problem

Clean water access is increasingly threatened by industrial pollutants, including heavy metals and toxic dyes. Simultaneously, aquaculture industries generate massive amounts of discarded shell waste that end up in landfills, creating a secondary environmental burden.

The PureFlow Solution

PureFlow hits two targets with one sustainable innovation. We give waste mussel shells a second life by converting them into an advanced, highly efficient filtration material. This bio-adsorbent captures harmful pollutants from contaminated water, promoting a true circular economy.

The Science & Roadmap

How It Works

Current Stage: Prototype Development

PureFlow has achieved critical validation and global recognition. Our immediate focus is on advancing prototype development, laboratory testing, pilot scaling, and securing certifications.

A scalable, safe, and scientifically rigorous process designed to maximize filtration efficiency while minimizing environmental footprint.

01
Collection

Waste mussel shells are ethically sourced and collected from industrial and aquaculture byproducts, preventing landfill accumulation.

02
Preparation

The raw material undergoes a precise, eco-friendly cleaning and preparation phase to isolate the optimal structural components.

03
Activation

The shell-based biomaterial is scientifically activated, significantly enhancing its surface area and porosity to create a highly efficient medium.

04
Adsorption & Scaling

Contaminated water passes through the material, capturing industrial dyes and heavy metals. We are currently scaling this into a deployable filtration prototype.
